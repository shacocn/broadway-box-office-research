Methods and limitations

Production OLS uses HC3 covariance and the exact formula: `log_gross ~ log_seats + Adapted + Revival + C(Year) + C(Music_Style) + Plot_Comedy + Plot_Tragedy + Plot_Romance + Plot_Mystery + Plot_Drama + Plot_Adventure + Plot_Crime + Plot_Biographical + Plot_Fantasy`. Categorical levels use sorted treatment coding; coefficient labels identify comparisons with the omitted baseline. Market extensions use month and year controls with HAC maximum lag 8, complete cases per formula, and chronological ordering. Their explicit formulas and matrix diagnostics are in results/market_extensions.csv. Seats are exp(log_seats), not independently verified capacity counts. Gross is nominal USD. No inflation adjustment is applied.

The 67 production aggregates cannot reconstruct the unavailable 4,972 show-week records or selection trail. These are observational associations, not causal effects. Budgets, casting, pricing and marketing are unmeasured. Labels are provisional. Hamilton is the only HipHop production. Removing it changes category support. R-squared across different samples is not a model ranking. Unemployment is missing for 244 of 501 weeks (48.7%), systematically by time. Annual tourism and monthly macro indicators repeat across weeks. Pandemic shutdown and reopening create structural breaks. Original market design matrices are unavailable; current market estimates are exploratory. Annual tourism with year indicators creates collinearity. HAC uses eight successive observed rows, which need not be consecutive calendar weeks across gaps. No missing values are imputed. Source redistribution permission remains unconfirmed.
